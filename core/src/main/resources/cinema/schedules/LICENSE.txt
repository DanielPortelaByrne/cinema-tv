classic-schedules.json

Which television shows ran together on US network television (1946 onward) and Canadian network television
(2003 to 2023): for each show the seasons it was on air, and for pairs of shows how many seasons they ran directly after
one another or on the same night of the same network.

Source: the season-by-season "network television schedule" pages of English Wikipedia
(for example "1989-90 United States network television schedule"), https://en.wikipedia.org, read with
scripts/schedules/build_dataset.py. The facts were taken from the schedule tables; show names are as Wikipedia gives them.
